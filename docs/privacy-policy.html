Privacy Policy

Swing: Marble Scale Puzzle (Android package com.edens.marblemaster)
Effective 22 October 2026 · Last updated 23 October 2026

Swing is a single-player puzzle game. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no trackers. This page explains the little data the game touches and what you can do about it.

1. The short version

  • Your high scores are stored only on your own device.
  • The game has no online features: it requests no permissions and sends no data anywhere.
  • We do not run any servers and we never receive your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, or advertising identifier.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves a top-ten high score list in its private app storage: the score and the date of each of your ten best runs, plus the best score shown as BEST during play. This is written with Android's SharedPreferences and is not readable by other apps.

You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings → Apps → Swing → Storage → Clear storage, or by uninstalling the game. No copy exists anywhere else.

3. No network access and no third-party services

The game does not request the Android INTERNET permission and makes no network requests. There is no sign-in, no leaderboard, no cloud save, and no third-party SDK in the app. Your scores never leave your device.

Google Play itself (the store app) may process install and usage information as part of distributing any Android app. That processing is governed by Google's privacy policy, not by this one.

4. What we do not collect

We do not collect, request, or store any of the following:

  • Name, email address, phone number, postal address, or any account credentials
  • Contacts, calendar, SMS, call logs, photos, files, or clipboard contents
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Camera, microphone, or body-sensor data
  • Advertising identifier, or any advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or attribution SDK
  • Device identifiers or usage statistics sent to us (we have no server to send them to)

5. Permissions

The game declares no Android permissions. It cannot access the network, storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or notifications.

6. Retention, deletion, and your choices

  • High scores: kept on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game.
  • Deletion request to us: email peterredens@gmail.com with the subject “Swing data deletion”. We hold no database of players, so in practice the only thing we can do is confirm that we hold nothing about you. We answer such requests within 30 days.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share personal data with anyone. There are no third-party SDKs, data brokers, or advertising partners in the app, and the app transmits nothing.

8. Security

The game contains no network code, so no score or other data is ever transmitted. Because no data reaches us, there is no database of ours that could be breached.

9. Children

Swing is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children; high scores stay on the device that played them. If you have any concerns, contact us at the address below.

10. Your rights (EEA, UK, and elsewhere)

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to export or erase on our side. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or a region with similar laws, you can still contact us and we will explain what we do and do not hold.

11. International users

The game can be used anywhere. It transmits no data, so there is no cross-border transfer of personal data by the app.

12. Changes to this policy

If the game starts using a new service or data type, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the “last updated” date at the top will change. Material changes will also be noted in the release notes on Google Play.
